# About

The projects and opinions here are personal projects and opinions, unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Professional stuff

I work on Digital Strategy for the Library of Congress, and I recently concluded a stint as Acting Chief of the [Digital Innovation Division at the Library of Congress (better known as LC Labs)](https://labs.loc.gov/).

Before that I was the first Managing Director of the [IIIF Consortium](https://iiif.io/).

Prior to that, I worked at the New York Public Library, as [Deputy Director of NYPL Labs](https://www.nypl.org/collections/labs), and as Associate Director of Digital Policy, among other roles. 

Find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hadro/) if you're interested in the details.

## Code and data

Most of what gets written up here recently has data behind it, and I try to post those things openly and publicly whenever and wherever possible:

- [GitHub](https://github.com/hadro) — pipelines, scripts, CSVs, site code, etc.
- [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/hadro) — datasets extracted from digitized collections, and some fine-tuned models I have been playing with.

Again: everything here is purely my own opinion, and does not reflect the opinion of my employer, past or present.
